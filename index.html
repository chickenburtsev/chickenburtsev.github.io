How a habit becomes Coco’s bonus

the loop
  1. Anchor itUp to 5 small habits, each tied to “after what”: after coffee, before sleep.
  2. One tapTap to check in, tap again to undo. One gentle reminder a day, max.
  3. Coco mirrors youCoco repeats the habit in her flat: drinks water, runs on the treadmill, reads.
  4. Bonus, not punishmentCoco earns bonuses. Nothing dies, nothing is taken away.
  5. Gentle streak“X of 7” plus a streak that forgives one miss a week.
  6. Evening reflectionClaude writes a short reflection on the day — then the loop starts again.

Research-backed product decisions

Three studies (~190 sources) on retention, conversion and health data shaped the design.

Forgiving streaks

One missed day barely affects habit formation, which takes ~66 days on average (18–254) — so one miss a week is forgiven.

Lally et al. · habit formation
“After X, I do Y” anchors

If-then plans substantially raise follow-through (effect size d≈0.65) — so every habit gets an anchor.

Gollwitzer · implementation intentions
No punishment mechanics

Penalties in habit apps backfire, and a broken streak without repair demotivates.

Habit-app studies review
Guest first, account later

Sign-up after the first good experience lifts engagement (+20% DAU in a measured case) — so the account is offered only after the first closed day.
